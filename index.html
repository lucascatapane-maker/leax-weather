Warning Thresholds

  • High Temperature: 35°C or greater.
  • High Pressure Altitude: 1,000 ft or greater.
  • High Density Altitude: 1,000 ft or greater.
  • High Crosswind: 15 knots or greater (relative to RWY 12/30).
  • High Gusts: Sustained wind ≥ 15 kts with gusts ≥ 25 kts.
  • Hazardous Weather: Thunderstorms (TS), Fog (FG), Snow (SN), or Heavy Rain (+RA).

These customized thresholds follow EASA/ICAO general guidelines to aid pilot situational awareness for light single-engine piston operations.
